Privacy policy

gta6-shorts · Last updated 19 November 2026

Who this applies to

gta6-shorts is a personal tool run by the operator of the Ctrl_MD YouTube channel. The only Google account it connects to is the operator's own.

Google and YouTube data

The app uses YouTube API Services with a single permission, youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the operator's own channel. It does not read, list, change, or delete any other YouTube data, and it does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the operator's own computer and server, is used only to upload videos, and is never sold, shared, or sent anywhere other than Google. The operator can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Twitch data

The app reads public chat on public Twitch streams to measure how quickly messages arrive. It keeps only counts and a few short sample messages per detected moment, for a limited time, to score and title clips. It does not build profiles or share chat data.

Cookies and tracking

This website and the app use no cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Third-party terms

By using YouTube features of this app you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the operator through the Ctrl_MD YouTube channel.
