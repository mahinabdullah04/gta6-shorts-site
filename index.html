gta6-shorts

A personal automation tool for the Ctrl_MD YouTube channel.

gta6-shorts watches public Twitch chat on live Grand Theft Auto VI streams. When a chat suddenly floods with reactions, it marks that moment, cuts a short clip from the stream's public recording, adds captions, formats it as a vertical video, and uploads it as a YouTube Short to the channel operator's own YouTube account. Every Short credits the streamer and links to the original stream.

The tool is used only by its operator to publish to their own channel. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups, or access to anyone else's YouTube data.
